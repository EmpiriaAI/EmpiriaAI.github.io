Core belief 核心判断

The defining constraint of the next phase of AI will not be models, algorithms, or compute alone, but the quality of the environments in which AI learns.

下一阶段 AI 的主要瓶颈，将不只是模型、算法或算力，而是高质量的学习环境。

Mission 使命

We build environments where AI can act, observe, verify, and learn from experience — enabling intelligence to move beyond existing data and accelerate progress in robotics, medicine, materials, and science.

构建让 AI 能够行动、观察、验证并从经验中学习的环境，推动智能突破已有数据的边界，加速机器人、医学、材料与科学的进步。

Empiria Labs is not a data vendor and not a training platform. What we build is the world in which experience can be earned.

Approach 方法

Experience is a loop,
not a dataset.

  1. 01

    Act

    行动

  2. 02

    Observe

    观察

  3. 03

    Verify

    验证

  4. 04

    Learn

    学习

Only when a system can take an action, observe its consequence, receive feedback and verify the outcome can it push past the boundary of what is already known — and turn that into capability it can actually use.

Stack 技术栈

Four stages,
one loop.

Roll out in a sandbox, record it under one schema, check the outcome against the world, then train on it — inside the same environment the evaluation runs in.

01 · Sandbox Act

Sandboxed worlds

Container-based environments rather than a virtual machine per task. Many pack onto one host and roll out in parallel, so an agent can act at scale instead of one episode at a time.

Environments — 24 in build

02 · Traces Observe

One trace schema

Every rollout lands as an observation paired with the action taken — a single step or a whole trajectory — in one format shared across both tracks.

Schema — two tracks, one format

03 · Verification Verify

Checkable outcomes

No environment ships without a programmatic check. The reward is computed from the state of the world after the act, not inferred from reading the transcript.

Measurement — outcome-verified

04 · Training Learn

Learning in-world

Fine-tune on the traces, then reinforce inside the very environments the evaluation runs in — so a gain measured here is a gain the agent actually earned.

Training — any open agent
